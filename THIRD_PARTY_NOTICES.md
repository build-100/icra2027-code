## Related research and adapted baselines

- [Graph-Based SLAM-Aware Exploration](https://github.com/bairuofei/Graph-Based_SLAM-Aware_Exploration)
  is a related graph-based exploration system. Consult its repository for the
  upstream implementation, MIT license, installation instructions, and citation.
- [CGE](https://github.com/bairuofei/CGE) is a related exploration system and a
  reference for greedy planning methods. A full upstream CGE working copy is not
  redistributed here; consult the upstream project for its terms and citation.
- QMIX and COMA are task-specific isolated implementations from the GEVD
  experiment code. They are not represented as the original upstream authors'
  software releases.

The planning baselines in this repository are adapted to GEVD's common graph
environment and evaluation objective. They are not verbatim executions of the
upstream ROS systems. Full external ROS workspaces, third-party Git histories,
and vendored runtime libraries are not included.
